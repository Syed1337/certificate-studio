# What's new

## 1.0.0 (6 October 2026)

First public release.

- 53 layouts in 172 colourways, including 18 new sports, primary, science, luxury, classic and modern layouts
- Start screen with **New certificate** and **Continue where you left off**
- Office-style floating format bar: click any part of the certificate to edit it
- Layout, colour and font dropdowns above the certificate (scroll the mouse wheel to flip through them)
- 24 professional font pairings grouped by style
- Several school logos; choose which one the watermark uses
- Medal branches (laurel, olive, oak, palm, wheat, leaves, swirl) and top ornaments (stars, crown, sparkles, pearls, rays)
- Grade and place medals (A*, A+, A, 1st, 2nd, 3rd and your own text)
- Give any student their own design; scroll through every student's certificate
- Clean printing through your PDF viewer (no headers or footers)
- Faster start and saving; the app closes properly and saves your work first
- Update check: the app tells you when a new version is available
